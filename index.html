Why Underdamping Occurs

Here, the damping constant β is smaller than the natural angular frequency ω₀ (β < ω₀). The restoring force (spring) is strong enough to repeatedly pull the mass past equilibrium, while the damping force progressively extracts kinetic energy, forming decaying sinusoidal oscillations contained within the exponential envelope ±A e-βt.
